[Gita 2.47] karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana | mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo 'stv akarmaṇi ||
Gloss (own words): You have a claim only to action, never to its fruits; do not make the fruit your motive, and do not cling to inaction. Core text on karma-yoga and dharma as duty without attachment to results.

[Gita 6.5] uddhared ātmanātmānaṁ nātmānam avasādayet | ātmaiva hy ātmano bandhur ātmaiva ripur ātmanaḥ ||
Gloss (own words): Lift yourself by your own self; do not let yourself sink. The self is its own friend and its own enemy. Speaks to self-effort and responsibility.

[Gita 4.7] yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānir bhavati bhārata | abhyutthānam adharmasya tadātmānaṁ sṛjāmy aham ||
Gloss (own words): Whenever dharma declines and adharma rises, I manifest myself. A foundational verse on dharma and divine intervention in the Gita.

[Isha 1] īśā vāsyam idaṁ sarvaṁ yat kiñca jagatyāṁ jagat | tena tyaktena bhuñjīthā mā gṛdhaḥ kasya svid dhanam ||
Gloss (own words): All this, whatever moves in the world, is pervaded by the Lord. Enjoy through renunciation; do not covet anyone's wealth. Opening of the Isha Upanishad on unity and non-possessiveness.

[Brihadaranyaka 1.3.28] asato mā sad gamaya | tamaso mā jyotir gamaya | mṛtyor mā amṛtaṁ gamaya ||
Gloss (own words): Lead me from untruth to truth, from darkness to light, from death to immortality. A prayer about knowledge and liberation.

[Yoga Sutra 1.2] yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ ||
Gloss (own words): Yoga is the stilling of the fluctuations of the mind. Patanjali's definition of yoga.
