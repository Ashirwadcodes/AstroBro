[Dosha] In Jyotish a dosha is a chart condition that tradition regards as a difficulty. Teachers differ on definitions, strength and cancellations. A dosha is not a verdict, and remedies are matters of belief that should never replace medical, legal or financial advice.

[Kaal Sarp dosha] Kaal Sarp dosha is said to arise when all seven planets (Sun to Saturn) lie on one side of the Rahu-Ketu axis. It is not found in the classical texts such as Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra but is widely taught in modern practice. Teachers name twelve types by the house of Rahu, and partial cases are said to be weaker. Some astrologers reject it entirely. AstroBro checks it by longitude.

[Pitra dosha] Pitra dosha is said to relate to ancestors, usually tied to the Sun or the 9th house being afflicted by Rahu, Ketu or Saturn. Traditional remedies are remembrance, charity and ancestral rites. Definitions vary widely.

[Guru Chandal yoga] Guru Chandal yoga is said when Jupiter is conjunct Rahu. Some read it as confusion about values, others as unconventional wisdom. It is a modern teaching name.

[Grahan dosha] Grahan dosha means an eclipse condition: the Sun or Moon conjunct Rahu or Ketu in the birth chart.

[Sade Sati] Sade Sati is the roughly seven and a half years when Saturn passes the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from the Moon. AstroBro computes it. Traditions view it as a time of testing and effort rather than only misfortune.

[Mangal dosha] Mangal (Kuja) dosha is said when Mars is in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house from the Lagna, and some also count from the Moon or Venus. It is used mainly for marriage matching and many cancellations are listed. AstroBro computes it.

[Kemadruma] Kemadruma yoga is said when the Moon has no planet in its sign or in the signs on either side. Many cancellations exist.

[Remedies] Common traditional remedies include mantra, charity, fasting, gemstones and worship. Gemstones can be costly and sellers sometimes pressure buyers; there is no evidence they change outcomes. Choose practices you find meaningful and do not spend money out of fear.
