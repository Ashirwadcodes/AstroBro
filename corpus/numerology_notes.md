[Numerology] Numerology assigns meaning to numbers derived from a birth date or a name. It is a traditional and modern belief system, not a science, and systems differ. Treat it as reflection, not prediction.

[Life Path number] The Life Path number comes from the full birth date: reduce day, month and year separately to a single digit, add them, and reduce again. Many teachers keep the master numbers 11, 22 and 33.

[Moolank and Bhagyank] Indian numerology uses the Moolank (root number: the birth day reduced to one digit) and the Bhagyank (destiny number: the whole date reduced to one digit). Numbers are linked to grahas: 1 Surya, 2 Chandra, 3 Guru, 4 Rahu, 5 Budha, 6 Shukra, 7 Ketu, 8 Shani, 9 Mangala.

[Name number] Pythagorean numerology maps letters A-Z to 1-9 in order. Chaldean numerology uses another mapping that never gives a letter the value 9. Destiny is the sum of all letters, Soul Urge the sum of vowels, Personality the sum of consonants.

[Meanings of numbers] 1 independence and leadership; 2 cooperation and sensitivity; 3 expression and creativity; 4 discipline and structure; 5 change and curiosity; 6 care and responsibility; 7 reflection and study; 8 ambition and authority; 9 compassion and completion. These are conventional associations, not facts.

[Personal year] The Personal Year adds the birth day, birth month and the current year, each reduced, giving a number from 1 to 9 that some use as the theme of the year.
