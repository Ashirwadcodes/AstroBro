[Palmistry] Palmistry (Hasta Samudrika in the Indian tradition) reads the shape of the hand, its lines and its mounts. It is a traditional belief practice without scientific support. It cannot diagnose health or predict events and should not guide medical, legal or financial choices.

[Main lines] Four lines most teachers start with: the Life line curves around the thumb base and is linked to vitality, not length of life; the Head line crosses the palm and is linked to thinking style; the Heart line runs under the fingers and is linked to emotional style; the Fate line runs up the centre and is linked to career direction. Not everyone has all four clearly.

[Mounts] Mounts are palm pads named for planets: Jupiter under the index finger (ambition), Saturn under the middle finger (discipline), Sun under the ring finger (creativity), Mercury under the little finger (communication), Venus at the thumb base (warmth), and Moon on the opposite side (imagination). A fuller mount is read as more of that quality.

[Hand shapes] Many systems sort hands into four element types: Earth (square palm, short fingers), Air (square palm, long fingers), Fire (long palm, short fingers), Water (long palm, long fingers). Labels differ between teachers.

[How to look] Compare both hands. Traditionally the dominant hand shows what one has done with life and the other the inherited pattern. Lines change over time, which the tradition itself notes.

[App limit] AstroBro does not read a photo of a palm. It can only explain the vocabulary above. Reading real lines needs a trained person and careful lighting.
